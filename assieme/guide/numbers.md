# Real numbers on what worked

From a real multi-agent team measured with `assieme report`, and from herdr's public tracker (to 7 November 2026).
Counts only; no team's work or messages are included.

- Wake-ups between agents took 74% of all input tokens in 24 hours.
- 60% of those wake-ups did no work.
- Wrong agent status is the most reported problem on herdr's tracker: 128 bug reports and 127 feature requests.

What this means for a design: fewer agents, fewer messages, long briefs in files, and reports instead of check-ins.
To see this Mac's own numbers, run `assieme report`.
